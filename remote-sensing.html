Remote Sensing Study Center

Remote sensing is the use of sensors to collect information about Earth without directly touching the object or area being studied.

This study center organizes Advik's Remote Sensing preparation into ten major topic areas.

Each topic will eventually have its own study page with explanations, examples, equations, diagrams, and original practice questions.

Remote Sensing Topics

Light & Remote Sensing

Properties of light, electromagnetic radiation, reflection, refraction, absorption, scattering, and important equations used in remote sensing.
