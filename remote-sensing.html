Remote Sensing — Observable Earth

Study the principles of remote sensing and learn how satellites, sensors, images, maps, and data are used to observe Earth's surface and atmosphere.

Use the study topics below to build your understanding step by step and practice interpreting remote sensing information.

1. Light

Properties of electromagnetic radiation, reflection, refraction, absorption, scattering, wavelength, frequency, irradiance, and important remote sensing equations.

Study Topic →

2. Sensors

Passive and active sensors, optical and thermal sensing, radar, SAR, InSAR, radar altimetry, and radar-derived elevation products.

Study Topic →

3. Satellite Orbits

Learn about LEO, MEO, high Earth orbit, polar and non-polar orbits, geostationary and geosynchronous satellites, and Kepler's Third Law.

Study Topic →

4. Image Resolution

Understand spatial, temporal, radiometric, and spectral resolution and how each affects remote sensing data.

Study Topic →

5. Mapping Fundamentals

Learn how to read maps and remote sensing displays, including scale, orientation, legends, labels, location, and dates.

Study Topic →

6. Measurements

Practice measuring distance, perimeter, area, altitude, elevation, slope, and gradient using remote sensing images and maps.

Study Topic →

7. Image Interpretation

Interpret natural-color, false-color, grayscale, thermal, radar, elevation, contour, and classified map displays.

Study Topic →

8. Earth Observation

Explore aerosols, albedo, surface temperature, reflectance, vegetation changes, geohazards, glacial movement, and other Earth processes.

Study Topic →

9. Land Cover Classification

Learn supervised and unsupervised classification and how remote sensing data can be used to identify and monitor different land-cover types.

Study Topic →

10. Human & Environmental Impacts

Study habitat loss, land-cover change, urbanization, deforestation, environmental changes, damage assessment, and monitoring.

Study Topic →
