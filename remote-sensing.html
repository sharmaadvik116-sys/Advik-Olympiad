Remote Sensing

Science Olympiad Division B

Season 2026–2027

Remote Sensing Study Center

Remote sensing is the use of sensors to collect information about Earth without directly touching the object or area being studied.

For Science Olympiad, Advik will study remote sensing concepts, sensors, satellite orbits, image characteristics, mapping, measurements, image interpretation, Earth observation, land-cover classification, and environmental impacts.
