# Sensors

Science Olympiad Division B — Remote Sensing

## Sensor Types

### Passive Sensors

Passive sensors detect naturally occurring electromagnetic radiation.

Remote Sensing Division B includes passive sensing in these wavelength ranges:

- Ultraviolet (UV)
- Visible
- Near-infrared (NIR)
- Shortwave infrared (SWIR)
- Thermal infrared

### Active Sensors

Active sensors send out energy and measure the response from Earth's surface or atmosphere.

Topics include:

- Radar backscatter
- Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)
- Interferometric SAR (InSAR)
- Radar altimetry
- Radar-derived elevation products

## Important Instruments

- Optical and infrared imagers
- Thermal infrared imagers
- Radiometers
- Radar altimeters
- Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)
- Digital Elevation Models (DEMs)

## Science Olympiad Focus

Be able to:

- Distinguish passive and active sensing.
- Identify the type of sensor being used.
- Understand what different wavelength ranges can reveal.
- Interpret basic radar and SAR information.
- Understand the basic purpose of InSAR.
- Interpret radar-derived elevation information.
- Connect sensors to the type of data they collect.

## Practice

Practice identifying sensors from descriptions, images, wavelength information, and remote sensing data.
