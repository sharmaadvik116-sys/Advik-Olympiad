# Satellite Orbits

Science Olympiad Division B — Remote Sensing

## Types of Orbits

- Low Earth Orbit (LEO)
- Medium Earth Orbit (MEO)
- High Earth Orbit
- Polar Orbit
- Non-polar Orbit
- Geostationary Orbit
- Geosynchronous Orbit

## What to Understand

Be able to:

- Identify different types of satellite orbits.
- Understand the basic purpose of each orbit.
- Compare polar and non-polar orbits.
- Understand the difference between geostationary and geosynchronous orbits.
- Connect an orbit type with the type of Earth observation it can support.

## Orbital Mechanics

The event includes:

- Kepler's Third Law

Focus on understanding the relationship between orbital period and orbital distance.

## Science Olympiad Focus

Questions may require interpreting satellite information and determining which type of orbit is being described.

Practice identifying orbit types from descriptions, diagrams, and satellite examples.
